Beyond the Meeting

Class 02 · The meeting

What can we confirm?

Give a clear update and agree on what happens next when one detail is still open.

Context

The visit is underway

It’s 10:00 a.m. The visitor has arrived. You planned to show how your team works, but access to one area is still pending. The visitor leaves at 11:00.

What can you still do together? What do you need to check first?

Read this line from Atlassian’s Project Poster and connect it to the visit.

“Validate what you already know and what you need to find out before moving forward.”

What do you know for sure? What decision would be risky right now?

Language for the goal

Ready. Pending. Next.

Ready

We’ve already prepared the overview.

We can go ahead with the briefing.

Pending

We’re still waiting for access.

We haven’t confirmed access yet.

Next

In the meantime, we can show you the process map.

I’ll get back to you by 10:30.

Say it with a new detail. Follow the teacher’s cues and keep the same opening.

Noticing

What can you promise?

“Access will be confirmed by 10:30.”

“I’ll get back to you by 10:30.”

  1. Which speaker promises a decision?
  2. Which speaker can keep their promise even if there’s no decision?
  3. How do still and yet help the visitor understand the situation?

Compare your answers aloud.

Recognize

Sort the message

Choose the purpose of each line.

Manipulate

Build the update

Put the lines in the order ready → pending → useful option → follow-up.

Decide

What would you say?

The visitor wants the final cost today. Your team has a draft estimate, but approval is still pending. You can send an update at 4:00 p.m.
  1. “We can share the draft estimate now. I’ll update you by four.”
  2. “You’ll have the approved figure by four.”
  3. “We’ll talk about it later.”

Choose the best response. Explain what makes the other two risky or unhelpful. Then add a question that checks what the visitor needs.

Production · Choose a case

Two roles. One plan.

In pairs: Choose a case and open only your own card. One-to-one: Play the host first. Your teacher plays the visitor. Swap for a new case.

Production · Agree and record

Make a decision together

  1. The host explains what is ready, what is pending and one thing you can do now.
  2. The visitor asks a follow-up question and explains their priority.
  3. Agree on an action, who will do it and when you’ll check in.
Show optional language support

We’ve already… · We’re still waiting for… · In the meantime, we can… · Would that work for you? · I’ll get back to you by…

Production · A new constraint

Now the visitor has to leave early

You have 10 minutes left together. The pending item will take at least 30 minutes. Keep the same case and revise your agreement.

What can you do now? What will happen after the visitor leaves?

Give a 30-second handover. Your listener should be able to repeat the action, owner and time.

Recap

I’m glad to have you here

Without looking back, tell your listener one thing that is ready, one thing that is pending and one next step.

How confident are you about doing this in a real meeting, from 1 to 5? What would move you up one point?
