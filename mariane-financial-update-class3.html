Useful language

Three expressions for checking financial information.

1reconcile the accounts

compare records and confirm that the information agrees

2an outstanding balance

an amount that still needs to be paid

3an overdue invoice

an invoice that has passed its payment date

Useful language

5supporting documents

6a discrepancy in the amount

a difference between two financial records
