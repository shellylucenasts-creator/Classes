CONTROLLED PRACTICE 1

What actually happened?

Choose the expression that matches each update.

  1. The vessel now leaves at 2 p.m., not 6 p.m. move up / push back
  2. The payment review changed from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. push back / call off
  3. The approval discussion will happen on Monday. put off / move up
  4. The service will not happen because of a safety alert. call off / take a rain check
  5. The client cannot meet today and suggests next Tuesday. take a rain check / call off
06 / 12
CONTROLLED PRACTICE 2

Rewrite the update

Keep the meaning. Change the wording.

  1. The vessel will leave four hours earlier.
  2. The payment review will happen at 3 p.m., not 1 p.m.
  3. We will discuss the exception on Monday, not today.
  4. The cleaning service will not happen.
