Language for the goal

Explain it in three steps

01 · Ocean

The water in part of the Pacific gets warmer than usual.

02 · Air

That can change the way air moves.

03 · Rainfall

It can affect rainfall in different places.

Analysis · Noticing

What changes in each sentence?

A. El Niño leads to heavy rain.

B. El Niño can lead to heavy rain.

Compare A and B. What does can tell us?

C. El Niño can lead to heavy rain in some places.

Compare B and C. What does in some places tell us?

A colleague asks, “So we’ll get heavy rain here?” How would you reply?

Controlled 1 · Recognize

What did you hear?

You’re waiting for a meeting to start. A colleague is explaining El Niño. Listen to three short comments. Match each comment to its meaning. You’ll hear them twice.

A. The amount of rain may change.

B. Different regions can be affected in different ways.

C. Part of the ocean is warmer than normal.
