Context · A company response

Two regional requests need a decision

The company has operations in Porto Alegre and Manaus. Weather conditions have affected employees and deliveries. Management can authorize only two emergency measures for next week.

Marina · Porto Alegre

She requests remote work and a different delivery route.

Rafael · Manaus

He requests smaller cargo loads and extended delivery deadlines.

The mission

Evidence before approval

Researcher A

Investigate the conditions around Porto Alegre. Decide which part of Marina’s request the evidence supports.

Researcher B

Investigate the conditions around Manaus. Decide which part of Rafael’s request the evidence supports.

Outcome: create a one-week response plan with only two approved measures, at least one for each region.

Language for the task

Evidence, urgency and action

has been affected by...

Transport has been affected by the low river level.

based on what we found...

Based on what we found, this request is urgent.

I think we should... because...

I think we should change the route because road access has become difficult.

These chunks help you move from information to a decision.
