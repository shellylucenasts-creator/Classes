Back to work

Camila: I felt nervous. I had to feed the baby, check my emails and handle a problem at work.
Laura: That must have been hard.

Feelings in context

What made her feel that way?

Before

I felt nervous before I went back to work.

I felt disappointed when I missed an important moment.

I felt embarrassed when I forgot a meeting.

After

Finishing everything made me feel relieved.

Solving the problem made me feel proud.

Seeing my colleagues again made me feel excited.

Empathy: I understand. · That must have been hard. · I’m happy for you.
Feelings + the reason.07 / 13
Analysis · Noticing

Which structure do you need?

I felt + feeling

Use it to name your emotion.

I felt nervous.

Situation + made me feel + feeling

Use it to connect the reason and emotion.

The first week made me feel proud.

Which sentence gives the reason?

I felt relieved.
Finishing the report made me feel relieved.
