02 · Language in context

THE VISITOR IS HERE.

Receptionist: Good morning. Can I help you?

Visitor: Yes! I'm looking for Nina. She's tall and has curly hair.

Receptionist: Oh, Nina! She's next to the window.

Visitor: Great, thank you!

Who is the visitor looking for? What does the receptionist say?

08 · Correct the mistake

THAT'S NOT THE PERSON!

Visitor: “She's next to the window.”

Choose someone in the photo who is not next to the window. Correct the visitor using that person's actual position.

09 · Free practice

WHO AM I LOOKING FOR?

Visitor

Choose a person from the photo. Describe their real appearance and ask where they are — do not point.

Receptionist

Listen to the clues. Identify the same person. Describe their actual position.

Switch roles. Choose a different person.

10 · Mission

FIND THE RIGHT PERSON

You're late for a meeting!

Role A · Visitor: Secretly choose a person shown in the lobby. Describe their real appearance. Ask where they are.

Role B · Receptionist: Listen and ask a clarifying question. Identify the person and say where they are in the photograph.

Switch roles and choose another person. The answer must match the photo.
