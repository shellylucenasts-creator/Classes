10 · Mission

FIND THE RIGHT PERSON

You have a visitor at Nova Office. Find their contact before the meeting starts.

ROLE A · VISITOR

You're looking for Anna.

She has long dark hair. She is friendly. You don't know where she is.

Ask the receptionist. Confirm you found the right person.

ROLE B · RECEPTIONIST

There are three people: Nina, Ben and Anna.

Nina is next to Ben. Ben is between Nina and Anna. Anna is in front of the window.

Help the visitor find the right person.

After completing the mission, switch roles and use a different person. The teacher can change the office layout.
