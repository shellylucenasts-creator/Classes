TEAM A · WHERE IS IT? · CLASS 1

WHERE IS SHE?

Find the right person in a busy office.

CAN DO · I can describe someone and say where they are.

01 · RECOVERY

WHO ARE WE TALKING ABOUT?

Look at the people. Describe two of them. Who has curly hair? Who looks friendly?

02 · LANGUAGE IN CONTEXT

THE VISITOR IS HERE

Visitor: Hi! I’m looking for Nina.

Receptionist: What does she look like?

Visitor: She’s tall and has long curly hair.

Receptionist: She’s near the window, between two men.

Visitor: Thank you!

Who is Nina? Where is she?

03 · FIND THE PEOPLE

FIND NINA

Nina is the tall woman with long curly hair, standing between two men near the large window.

Find Nina. Who is next to her? Where is the reception desk?

04 · USEFUL LANGUAGE

WHERE ARE THEY?

next to

Nina is next to a man in a blue suit.

between

Nina is between two men.

near

Nina is near the window.

in front of

The visitor is in front of the reception desk.

05 · NOTICE

WHAT CHANGES?

She is near the window.

Is she near the window?

He is next to Nina.

Is he next to Nina?

What changes when we ask a question?

06 · RECOGNIZE

FIND AND DESCRIBE

Look at the photo and find:

  1. The tall woman with curly hair.
  2. The man on her left, wearing a blue suit.
  3. The man on her right, holding a laptop.
  4. The receptionist behind the desk.

Say where each person is. Use next to / between / near / behind / in front of.

07 · BUILD THE QUESTION

ASK THE RECEPTIONIST

Build six questions. Tap the words in the right order.

08 · CORRECT THE INFORMATION

THAT'S NOT NINA!

Visitor: “Nina is at the reception desk.”

Look again. Is that correct? Say where Nina really is.

Possible answer: No, she isn’t. She’s near the window, between two men.

09 · FREE PRACTICE

WHO AM I LOOKING FOR?

VISITOR

Choose a person in the photo. Describe them without pointing.

Ask: Where is he / she?

RECEPTIONIST

Listen carefully. Identify the person and say where they are.

Switch roles.

10 · FINAL MISSION

FIND THE RIGHT PERSON

ROLE A · VISITOR

You are looking for Nina. She is tall and has curly hair. Ask for her location.

Confirm that you have found the correct person.

ROLE B · RECEPTIONIST

Use the lobby photo. Help the visitor find Nina.

Give her position clearly. Then switch roles and choose someone else.

11 · RECAP

WHAT CAN YOU SAY NOW?

1. Where is Nina?

2. Is Nina between two men?

3. Where is the receptionist?

4. Correct this: “Nina is behind the desk.”

1. Near the window. 2. Yes, she is. 3. Behind the reception desk. 4. No, she isn’t. She’s near the window.

I’m glad to have you here!
