Language in context

A difficult interview question

Interviewer: Tell me about a mistake you made at work.
Candidate: In my first month, I sent a report with the wrong amount. I felt embarrassed and disappointed.
I corrected it and explained the problem. My manager said I handled it well. That made me feel relieved and proud.
Interviewer: I understand. That must have been hard.
First understand the experience.03 / 13
Understand the story

What changed?

1. What was the problem?

The candidate missed the interview.
She sent a report with the wrong amount.

2. What did she do?

She corrected it and explained the problem.
She ignored the report.

3. How did her feelings change?

Proud → nervous → disappointed
Embarrassed → disappointed → relieved → proud
Situation → action → emotional change.04 / 13
