WHERE IS IT? · Class 2 · A1

WHAT IS SHE WEARING?

Find the right person using their clothes and location.

CAN DO · I can identify a person by their clothes and say where they are.

01 · Recovery

WHO IS WHERE?

Look at the people. Describe and locate them.

02 · Language in context

A VISITOR NEEDS HELP

Visitor: Excuse me. I’m looking for Nina.

Receptionist: What does she look like?

Visitor: She’s tall. She has curly hair.

Receptionist: What is she wearing?

Visitor: A light blazer and beige pants.

Receptionist: She’s near the window, between two men.

Find Nina in the photograph. How do you know it is her?

Useful chunk: He’s / She’s wearing… (Learn it as one expression.)

05 · Guided noticing

WHAT CHANGES?

She’s near the window.

She’s wearing a light blazer.

Is she near the window?

What is she wearing?

“She is” tells us her location. “She’s wearing” tells us about her clothes.

08 · Correct the description

SOMETHING IS WRONG

Visitor: “Ben is wearing a black jacket.”

Look at Ben. Correct the visitor.

No, he isn’t. He’s wearing a green sweater.
09 · Free practice

DESCRIBE AND FIND

Person A: Secretly choose someone in the photo. Describe their hair and clothes. Don’t say their name.

Person B: Find the person. Ask where they are and confirm.

Switch roles and choose a different person.

10 · Team mission

FIND THE RIGHT COLLEAGUE

The meeting starts in five minutes!

The visitor must find the right colleague before the meeting.

VISITOR

Find a tall woman with curly hair. Ask about her location and confirm her clothes.

RECEPTIONIST

Identify Nina in the photo. Describe her clothes and position without pointing.

Switch roles. On round 2, locate the man in the green sweater.
