Where is it? · Final project · A1

The meeting with Nina

Find the restaurant. Find Nina. Meet your new business partner.

01 · Your mission

A first meeting

You work in an office.

Your office will work with a new company.

Nina works for that company.

She is waiting at Corner Bistro.

You don’t know Nina.

You don’t know where the restaurant is.

Your mission: Ask people at the office. Find the restaurant. Ask the receptionist. Find Nina!

02 · Success criteria

What do you need to do?

1. At the office

Ask people: “Where is Corner Bistro?”

2. In the city

Look at the map. Find Corner Bistro.

3. At the restaurant

Ask: “Where is Nina?” Find her.

Work in pairs. Ask and answer. Do not show your secret card.

03 · Recovery · Look and speak

What do you remember?

Observe the photo. Try to remember the language from your previous classes.

04 · Before the mission

Build your team strategy

Partner A

What can you ask to find a place?

Partner B

What can you ask to find a person?

Prepare together. No extra vocabulary is introduced in this project.

05 · Task 1 · Office information gap

Ask your coworker

Visitor · Partner A

You want to find Corner Bistro. You do not know where it is.

Ask for the street, a landmark and the distance.

Only the coworker may open the information card.

Coworker · Partner B

You know the location. Listen to your partner's questions and answer naturally.

Your information

Corner Bistro is on Main Street, on the corner of Main Street and Oak Street. It is about two blocks away from the office.

There is a pharmacy next to the office.

Then swap roles and ask again without looking at the card.

06 · Task 2 · Find the restaurant

Use the city map

Work in pairs. Find Corner Bistro from your office.

Say where it is using two location clues.

Do both partners agree?

07 · Your next stop

Welcome to Corner Bistro

You found the restaurant!

But you have never met Nina.

There are several customers here, and Nina is waiting for you.

Who should you ask?

08 · Task 3 · Restaurant information gap

Talk to the receptionist

Guest · Partner A

You are here to meet Nina, the representative of a new partner company.

Ask the receptionist where Nina is and how you can recognize her.

Don't read the receptionist's card.

Receptionist · Partner B

Welcome the guest. Answer only the questions they ask.

Your information

Nina is near the window, next to a plant.

She is tall, has curly hair and is wearing a light blazer and beige pants.

She is waiting for two visitors from the office.

Swap roles. Try the conversation again with less support.

09 · Task 4 · Identify Nina

Which person is Nina?

You have the receptionist's description.

Explain how you recognize Nina. Include her appearance, clothes and location.

Now agree on one person.

Nina — profile photo

Use your own English first.

What does she look like?

What is she wearing?

Where is she waiting?

10 · Final performance · Two rounds

Complete the whole mission

Round 1

Partner A: visitor.

Partner B: coworker, then receptionist.

Complete the office and restaurant conversations.

Round 2

Partner B: visitor.

Partner A: coworker, then receptionist.

Switch roles and complete the mission again.

No reading a full script. Ask, listen, clarify, and find Nina.
