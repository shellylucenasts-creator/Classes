2 · TWO VERY DIFFERENT OPINIONS

Who do you agree with more?

Reader A ★★★★★
I really enjoy Colleen Hoover's books. They're easy to read, emotional, and they keep me interested. I understand why so many people love them. Rated 4 out of 5
Reader B ★★★★★
I don't really understand the hype around her books. The stories can be entertaining, but sometimes the characters and relationships feel unrealistic to me. Rated 2 out of 5
  • Who do you agree with more?
  • What part do you agree with?
  • What would you disagree with?
