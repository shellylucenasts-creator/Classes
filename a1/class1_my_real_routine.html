A day in Daniel's life

Daniel just joined a tech company. His coworkers know who he is, but they don't know what his normal day looks like.

What do you think Daniel does every day?

Hi! I'm Daniel. I work in IT. On weekdays, I usually wake up at 7 a.m. I get up, take a shower and have breakfast. I start work at 9 a.m.

In the morning, I check my emails and work on different projects. I have lunch at around 12:30. In the afternoon, I usually have meetings and finish my tasks. I finish work at 6 p.m.

After work, I sometimes play video games or go to the gym. At night, I study English and watch a series. I usually go to bed at around 11 p.m.

Teacher notes

Focus

The class must remain reading-first. Do not turn the lesson into a Simple Present grammar lesson.

Progression

  • Recover
  • Understand
  • Find evidence
  • Notice language
  • Manipulate
  • Personalize
  • Transfer
  • Produce

Vocabulary

José should encounter the target chunks multiple times across different tasks instead of receiving more vocabulary.
