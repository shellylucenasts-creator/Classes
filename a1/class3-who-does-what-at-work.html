3 · READING

MEET THE TEAM

Marina

Data Analyst

She starts work at 9. She checks the dashboards every morning. She works with sales data and prepares reports. She finishes work at 6.

Lucas

IT Support

He starts work at 8. He checks support tickets and fixes computer problems. He helps coworkers with their laptops. He finishes work at 5.

Camila

Project Manager

She starts work at 8:30. She organizes meetings, checks deadlines and talks to clients. She finishes work at 6.

Who works with data? Who fixes computer problems? Who organizes meetings? When do they start and finish? Find evidence in the text.
